## List of models with centro symmetric shapes 
## and Iqabc(qa,qb,qc) already implemented.

Computation of the amplitude Fqabc(qa,qb,qc) is the squareroot of the intensity:

Fqabc(qa,qb,qc)=sqrt(Iqabc(qa,qb,qc))

### superball model: 
* gives a sphere with p=1 
* gives a cube with p=infinity

### triaxial ellipsoid model:
* gives a sphere for Ra=Rb=Rc (radius_equat_minor=Major equatorial radius=Polar radius)

### elliptical cylinder model:
* gives a cylinder (circular cross-section) with axis_ratio=1 

### rectangular_prism:
* gives a cube with b2a_ratio=c2a_ratio=1

### parallelepiped:
* gives a cube with length_a=length_b=length_c

### truncated_octahedron:
* gives a regular octahedron with b2a_ratio=c2a_ratio=1
* gives a regular cuboctahedron with b2a_ratio=c2a_ratio=1 and t=0.5
